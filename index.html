Australian accommodation, 2018–2026

How evenly has accommodation demand recovered?

Australia’s accommodation recovery varied across accommodation types, destinations and visitor markets. Explore where visitor nights returned, how accommodation preferences differed and when hotels were busiest.

Section 1

How did accommodation demand recover?

Recovery differed across domestic accommodation types.

Hotels exceeded their 2019 demand

Hotel nights rose 12.4% between 2019 and 2024. Totals increased from 101.0 million to 113.5 million.

Three categories remained below 2019

Rented stays fell 2.7%; caravan and camping fell 4.7%. Friends or relatives’ stays remained 7.0% lower.

The dashed line marks each category’s own 2019 level. Source: Tourism Research Australia, National Visitor Survey.

Comparability This comparison ends in 2024. Domestic Tourism Statistics replaced the survey in 2025. The changed collection method prevents directly joining these series.
