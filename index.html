Australian accommodation, 2019–2026

How evenly has accommodation demand recovered?

Australia’s accommodation recovery differed by accommodation type, location and season.

The index compares each accommodation type with its own 2019 level. A value of 100 means visitor nights have returned to the 2019 level.

Series break: the National Visitor Survey ended in December 2024. Domestic Tourism Statistics began in 2025 using a new collection method, so the two series should not be connected directly.

Darker cells represent more visitor nights. Values are displayed directly so the chart does not depend only on colour.

Each circle represents a neighbourhood. Larger circles contain more short-term rental listings, while darker circles have higher median nightly prices.

Thicker routes represent more international trips to Australia. Blue origin points grew from 2025, while orange indicates a decline. Values represent thousands of trips.

Blue points show 2026 and grey points show 2025. Labels indicate percentage change from 2025. Values represent international visitor nights rather than the number of visitors.

Rank 1 represents the destination with the most domestic visitor nights in hotels and similar accommodation. Crossings show destinations overtaking one another. Rankings do not show the size of the difference, so hover over a point to see the actual visitor-night value.

Curves show the distribution of recorded nightly prices. Listings above AUD 1,000 are excluded from the displayed distributions for readability. Dashed lines show each city’s median price.

Band thickness represents domestic visitor nights, not vertical position. “Other” combines own property, guest house/B&B and miscellaneous accommodation. Values are shown in millions of visitor nights.

Column width represents each state’s total domestic visitor nights. Segment height represents each accommodation type’s share, meaning segment area represents the number of visitor nights. “Other/own property” combines other accommodation, own property and guest houses or B&Bs. The five largest states by visitor nights are shown.

Orange indicates occupancy below the same month in 2018–19, while blue indicates occupancy above that baseline. Each darker band adds another 20 percentage points of change. Hover to see the exact occupancy rate. The Australian Accommodation Monitor covers commercial accommodation establishments with at least 10 rooms, so these results are broader than hotels alone.
